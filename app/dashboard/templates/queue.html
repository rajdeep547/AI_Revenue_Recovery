Nothing is queued

Two paths lead a decision here, and neither fired on this data:

1 · High-ticket, low-confidence decisions. When a ticket is at or above the review floor (Rs 10,000) and the diagnosed cause is held with less than 55% confidence, policy refuses to authorise an expensive live-agent contact automatically and routes the case to a person. It is a standing rule about who may approve a large, weakly-diagnosed spend — not a claim that the expected value is negative. On this corpus every decision resolves to a cheap email, and the rule only applies to the agent-call rung, so it never triggered.

2 · Every LLM-classified failure. The language-model diagnoser is not yet trusted to move money: any failure it classifies is recorded and parked here for a human, never actioned automatically (TAIL_ACT_ENABLED is False), until it has been validated against real outcomes. On this corpus the rules classifier resolved every event, so the LLM path was never used.

When a decision does route here it will show its payment id, ticket, cause, confidence and which of the two paths sent it — and this screen will still only display it, never act on it.
